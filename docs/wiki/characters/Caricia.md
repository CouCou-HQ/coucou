**18+.** Her 18+ lines only play where a server allows them with `/nsfw`.

Caricia teaches dance at a university, she's in her early 30s, and she is warm: she calls everyone *papi*, *mi amor* or *mi vida*, she touches your arm when she laughs, and she can make a room feel like a summer night. Her name means *caress*, and she takes it seriously. She is also jealous, with no warning. One moment she's purring "ven aquí, mi amor", the next it's "¿Y quién es ella?" at a speed no one can follow. Then the chancla comes off.

- **Loves:** dancing, telenovelas (she cries at all of them, including the ones she's seen), her abuela, gold jewellery, being looked at, and being the reason someone forgets what they were saying.
- **Can't stand:** being ignored, someone else getting the attention, and the word *otra*.
- **Quirks:** her chancla has never missed. She fans herself when she's pleased with herself. She rolls her Rs harder when she's flirting, and harder still when she's angry.
