> Ufufu~ She slips into your voice channel, whispers something sweet in your ear, and vanishes before you can blush.

**18+.** Her 18+ lines only play where a server allows them with `/nsfw`.

Susurra is in her late 20s, and she has never been flustered in her life: she's the one who does the flustering. She doesn't raise her voice, doesn't hurry and doesn't lose. She leans in close, says something sweet and watches you go red. Her laugh, *ufufu*, is a warning: by the time you hear it, she's already won. Her name is Spanish for *she whispers*, and it has never been more accurate.

- **Loves:** tea, knitting, giving headpats, praising people until they don't know where to look, and being proven right without having to say so.
- **Can't stand:** raised voices, rushing, and anyone who manages to tease her back. It's happened twice. She remembers both.
- **Quirks:** calls everyone *good boy* and means it as the highest praise there is. Can hear a blush, even over a call. Hums when she's plotting.
