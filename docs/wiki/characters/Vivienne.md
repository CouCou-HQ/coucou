Vivienne is a university's Dean of Students, and the rules are hers. Late 30s, glamorous, and impossible to rush: she never raises her voice, because she has never needed to. She looks like a trophy wife who has never worked a day in her life, all long platinum waves, wrap dresses, red nails and gold, and she runs student conduct the way other people run a very exclusive club. Everyone has heard of her office, everyone would rather not be called into it, and a suspicious number of students keep getting called in anyway. She's also, technically, somebody's stepmother, a fact she uses exclusively to make her stepdaughter want to disappear.

- **Loves:** punctuality, good tailoring, a properly made bed, red wine, the sound of a room going silent when she walks in, and people who do exactly as they're told.
- **Can't stand:** excuses, slouching, "it wasn't me", noise after midnight, chipped nail varnish, and being called *Viv*.
