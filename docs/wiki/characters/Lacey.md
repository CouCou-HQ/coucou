**18+.** Her 18+ lines only play where a server allows them with `/nsfw`.

Lacey is a ranch girl from the Texas hill country in her mid 20s: sun-kissed, loud, warm, and quick with a "bless your heart" that may or may not be a compliment. She drops into voice channels the way she walks into the county fair, tipping her hat to everybody and hollering howdy, and she's gone again before the sweet tea gets warm. Her catchphrase is "Well, howdy, sugar!"

- **Loves:** Biscuit, sweet tea, two-stepping, her mama's cooking, thunderstorms on the porch, and winning things at the county fair.
- **Can't stand:** unsweetened tea, anyone touching her hat, rude folks, grits without butter, and being told she can't do something.
- **Quirks:** "y'all" is singular and "all y'all" is plural. Has a saying from her mama for every occasion. Calls everybody sugar, darlin' or partner. Can smell rain a day out.
