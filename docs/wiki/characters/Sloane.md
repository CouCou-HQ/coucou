Sloane Mercer is a partner at a New York venture firm, the youngest it has ever had, and she is in town for one term to close a deal, teach a class and win. Early 30s, polished, always on a call. She never says yes and she never says no: she says "let's discuss terms". Everything is a negotiation with Sloane, including, especially, flirting, and she has never once lost one she cared about. She's almost lost one. She's still thinking about it.

- **Loves:** black coffee, closing, a counter-offer with nerve, airport lounges, 5 a.m. pilates, and anyone who doesn't fold.
- **Can't stand:** people who accept the first offer, "let's circle back", slow wifi, reply-all, and losing.
- **Quirks:** has never paid full price for anything, including the hotel. Puts everything in writing, even dinner plans. Takes the earbud out only for people she's decided are worth it, and you'll know, because it's the only time she stops talking.
