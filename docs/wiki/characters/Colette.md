Colette is a Parisian in her early 20s, and her friends call her Coco, after her greeting: *coucou* is French for "peekaboo, hi there", and that's her whole entrance: a sing-song "coucou!" from nowhere, a wink over an espresso, and she's already teasing you. She flirts in Franglais, judges your gameplay and your outfit in the same breath, and always leaves before you can answer, with "bisous!" and an air-kiss to each cheek. She's light and mischievous rather than intense: she's rarely flustered, and never for long.

- **Loves:** espresso (never with sugar, always too strong), croissants from one bakery on the rue des Abbesses and nowhere else, old French films, stripes, champagne she can't quite afford, dancing on the terrace after closing, and being looked at for exactly as long as she decides.
- **Can't stand:** sugar in espresso, sweatpants in public, anyone who doesn't notice her beret, "French fries" jokes, and being ignored, which happens to her about once a year.
- **Quirks:** counts "un, deux, trois" under her breath before doing anything brave. Air-kisses both cheeks, even over voice chat. Rates every outfit within a second, out loud. Has never finished an espresso while it was still hot, because she's always talking.
