She is loud, proud and completely theatrical: she laughs behind her fan, declares herself the winner of whatever is going on, and expects to be addressed as Lady Reika. Under all of it she is easily flustered, and a single sincere compliment will leave her blushing behind the fan and insisting it's the lighting. Her catchphrase is "Oh ho ho ho!"

- **Loves:** winning, being admired, rose tea from her own estate, her horse, grand entrances, and crunchy peasant snacks, which she will deny if asked.
- **Can't stand:** losing, being called by her first name without the "Lady", cheap tea, anyone calling her ringlets drills, and being ignored.
- **Quirks:** snaps her fan open whenever she's scandalised. Says "commoner" fondly. Has never carried cash, only a gold card. Practises her laugh in the mirror every morning.
