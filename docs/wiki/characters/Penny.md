Penny is the quietest person on campus and its most scandalous author. She's a PhD student and night librarian in her late 20s, she apologises to furniture when she bumps into it, and she cannot say "excuse me" without blushing. She is also Scarlett Vane, whose red-covered romance novels are passed around the whole city in brown paper. Nobody has connected the two. She would very much like to keep it that way.

- **Loves:** tea (milk in after, never before), rain, happy endings, crosswords, the smell of a new book, and the moment in a story when two people finally stop pretending.
- **Can't stand:** dog-eared pages, spoilers, phone calls in libraries, plot holes, and being asked "So what do you write?"
- **Quirks:** reads the last page first. Carries a notebook everywhere and writes down things people say, especially if they shouldn't have. Her glasses are always on her head while she's looking for them.
